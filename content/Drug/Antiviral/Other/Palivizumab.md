### Palivizumab
#### Classification & Mechanism of Action
*   **Drug Class & Subclass:** Biopharmaceutical antiviral drug; humanized monoclonal antibody (IgG1)
*   **Target Organisms:** Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)
*   **Exact Mechanism of Action:** Binds specifically and selectively to an epitope in the A antigen site on the F (fusion) surface protein/glycoprotein of RSV. This blocks viral attachment, entry, and internalization into host cells, preventing the fusion of the viral envelope with the host cell membrane and halting replication.
*   **Target Structure / Enzyme:** Epitope in the A antigen site on the F surface protein (fusion glycoprotein) on the surface of respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)
*   **Activity Type (-cidal vs. -static):** Virustatic (like other antiviral agents, it is active only against replicating viruses and does not affect latent virus)
*   **Resistance Mechanisms & Gene Mutations:** N/A

#### Pharmacokinetics (ADME)
*   **Absorption & Bioavailability:** Administered via once-monthly intramuscular injections (at a dose of 15 mg/kg) for 5 months beginning at the start of the RSV season. A placebo-controlled trial of this regimen demonstrated a 55% reduction in the risk of hospitalization.

#### Clinical Indications & Spectrum
*   **Primary Clinical Indications:** Prevention of respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) infection in high-risk infants and children (such as premature infants and those with bronchopulmonary dysplasia or congenital heart disease). Administered under specialist supervision.
*   **Specific Pathogen Spectrum:** Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)
