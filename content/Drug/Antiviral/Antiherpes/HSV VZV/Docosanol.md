*   **Exceptions to the Rule:**
    - The Entry Inhibition Exception: While the majority of established therapies for Herpes simplex virus (HSV) are nucleoside or nucleotide analogs (such as acyclovir, valacyclovir, and famciclovir) that act intracellularly to inhibit viral DNA polymerase and nucleic acid synthesis, docosanol is a notable exception that acts extracellularly at the cell surface to block viral attachment and host cell entry.
