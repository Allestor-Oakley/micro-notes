**CXCL12 (SDF1):** Binds to the **CXCR4** receptor. It mediates [[B Cell|B-cell]] and [[T-Cell|T-cell]] migration into [[Lymph Nodes]], and directs IgG-secreting plasma cells to migrate into the bone marrow. It is also secreted by specialized cortical thymic epithelial cells to draw committing thymocytes to the outer thymic cortex.
