**CCL25 (TECK):** Binds to the **CCR9** receptor. It is secreted by intestinal epithelial cells to attract gut-homing lymphocytes, including IgA-secreting plasma cells and effector [[T-Cell|T cells]], into the lamina propria of the intestine. It is also produced in the inner thymic cortex to draw early T-cell progenitors into the [[Thymus]].
