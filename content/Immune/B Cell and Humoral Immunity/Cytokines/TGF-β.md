Highly pleiotropic. It inhibits T-cell proliferation and effector functions, promotes the differentiation of both Th17 and Treg cells, inhibits B-cell proliferation while forcing IgA production, inhibits macrophage activation (stimulating angiogenesis instead), and increases collagen synthesis in fibroblasts.
