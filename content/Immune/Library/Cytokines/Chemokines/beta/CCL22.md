**CCL21 (SLC):** Binds to the **CCR7** receptor. Like [[CCL19]], it is produced by FRCs and the high endothelial venules (HEVs). It serves to draw naive T cells and dendritic cells into the T-cell zones of lymph nodes, and it also directs activated B cells toward the T-cell boundary during B-T cell interactions.
