**CCL21 (SLC):** Binds to the **CCR7** receptor. Like CCL19, it is produced by FRCs and the high endothelial venules (HEVs). It serves to draw naive [[T-Cell|T cells]] and [[Dendritic Cell]] into the T-cell zones of [[Lymph Nodes]], and it also directs activated [[B Cell|B cells]] toward the [[T-Cell|T-cell]] boundary during B-T cell interactions.
