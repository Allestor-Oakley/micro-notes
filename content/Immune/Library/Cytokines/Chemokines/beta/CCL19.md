**CCL19 (MIP-3β/ELC):** Binds to the **CCR7** receptor. Produced primarily by [[T-Cell|T-cell]] zone fibroblastic reticular cells (FRCs) and lymphatic endothelial cells, it directs the migration of naive [[T-Cell|T cells]], [[B Cell|B cells]], and activated [[Dendritic Cell|dendritic cells]] into the parafollicular T-cell zones of [[Lymph Nodes]].
