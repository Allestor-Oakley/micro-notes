**CCL19 (MIP-3β/ELC):** Binds to the **CCR7** receptor. Produced primarily by T-cell zone fibroblastic reticular cells (FRCs) and lymphatic endothelial cells, it directs the migration of naive T cells, B cells, and activated dendritic cells into the parafollicular T-cell zones of lymph nodes.
