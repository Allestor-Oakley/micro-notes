**CXCL13 (BCA-1):** Binds to the **CXCR5** receptor. It is produced by B-cell zone FRCs and follicular dendritic cells (FDCs). It acts as the master chemokine to draw naive B cells and T follicular helper (Tfh) cells into the lymphoid follicles (B-cell zones) to initiate humoral immune responses.
