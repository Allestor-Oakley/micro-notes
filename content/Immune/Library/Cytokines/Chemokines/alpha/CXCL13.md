**CXCL13 (BCA-1):** Binds to the **CXCR5** receptor. It is produced by B-cell zone FRCs and follicular [[Dendritic Cell|dendritic cells]] (FDCs). It acts as the master chemokine to draw naive [[B Cell|B cells]] and T follicular helper (Tfh) cells into the lymphoid follicles (B-cell zones) to initiate humoral immune responses.
