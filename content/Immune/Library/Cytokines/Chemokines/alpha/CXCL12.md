**CXCL12 (SDF1):** Binds to the **CXCR4** receptor. It mediates B-cell and T-cell migration into lymph nodes, and directs IgG-secreting plasma cells to migrate into the bone marrow. It is also secreted by specialized cortical thymic epithelial cells to draw committing thymocytes to the outer thymic cortex.

**CXCR4** are uniquely co-opted to act as essential coreceptors for the entry of the human immunodeficiency virus ([[HIV]]) into host cells.
