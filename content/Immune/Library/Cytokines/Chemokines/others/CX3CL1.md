**CX3CL1 (Fractalkine):** Binds to the **CX3CR1** receptor. It mediates the recruitment of [[T Cell|T cells]], [[NK Cell|NK cells]], and [[Monocyte|monocytes]]. Furthermore, the CX3CR receptor is utilized by specialized antigen-sampling cells in the gut to maintain barrier integrity while extending dendrites into the lumen.
