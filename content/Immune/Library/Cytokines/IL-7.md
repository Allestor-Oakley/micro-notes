**Interleukin-7 (IL-7):** Crucial for early lymphocyte development, driving the proliferation of early [[T Cell|T-cell]] and [[B Cell|B-cell]] progenitors. It also functions peripherally to ensure the survival of naive and memory T lymphocytes.
