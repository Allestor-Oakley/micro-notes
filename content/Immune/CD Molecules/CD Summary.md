## The T-Cell Arsenal: Receptors, Co-receptors, and Checkpoints
This group includes molecules directly involved in T-cell antigen recognition, activation, and critical regulatory checkpoints.
- **CD3 (γ, δ, ε) & CD247 (TCR ζ chain):** These molecules associate to form the T-cell antigen receptor (TCR) complex. They all contain Immunoreceptor Tyrosine-based Activation Motifs (ITAMs) in their cytoplasmic tails, which are essential for cell surface expression and signal transduction upon antigen binding.
- **CD4 & CD8 (α, β):** The fundamental T-cell coreceptors. CD4 (55 kD) binds to MHC class II molecules and is a receptor for HIV, while CD8 (expressed as an αα homodimer or αβ heterodimer, 34 kD each) binds to MHC class I molecules. Both are crucial for thymocyte development and antigen-induced T-cell activation.
- **CD2 (LFA-2):** A 50 kD adhesion molecule on T cells and NK cells that binds CD58; it plays a role in T-cell activation and cytolytic lysis.
- **CD28:** The primary receptor for costimulatory molecules CD80 (B7-1) and CD86 (B7-2). It is an Ig superfamily homodimer expressed on all CD4+ and most CD8+ T cells in humans, providing the essential "signal 2" for activation.
- **CD154 (CD40 Ligand):** A TNF superfamily homotrimer expressed on activated CD4+ T cells that binds CD40 to activate B cells, macrophages, and endothelial cells.
- **Inhibitory Checkpoints (CD152 & CD279):** CD152 (CTLA-4) binds CD80/CD86 on antigen-presenting cells to mediate the suppressive function of regulatory T cells and inhibit T-cell responses. CD279 (PD-1) binds PD-L1 (CD274) and PD-L2 (CD273) to similarly inhibit T-cell activation.
- **Activating Checkpoints (CD278 & CD134):** CD278 (ICOS) binds ICOS ligand (CD275) to costimulate T cells and aid in T follicular helper (Tfh) cell development. CD134 (OX40) binds CD252 (OX40 ligand) for T-cell costimulation.

## The B-Cell Arsenal: Signaling, Coreceptors, and Survival
This group governs B-cell development, antigen receptor signaling, and long-term survival.
- **The B-Cell Coreceptor Complex (CD19, CD21, CD81):** This triad delivers vital signals that synergize with the B-cell antigen receptor. CD21 (CR2) specifically functions as the receptor for the complement fragment C3d and the Epstein-Barr virus.
- **CD79a (Igα) & CD79b (Igβ):** These dimerize to form the signaling component of the B-cell receptor complex. Like CD3 in T cells, they possess ITAMs required for signal transduction and surface expression.
- **CD20:** A 33-37 kD MS4A family protein with an unknown natural function, but it is highly significant clinically as the target for B cell-depleting antibodies.
- **CD22 (Siglec-2):** An inhibitory receptor with an Immunoreceptor Tyrosine-based Inhibition Motif (ITIM) that regulates B-cell activation and acts as an adhesion molecule.
- **CD40:** A TNFR superfamily homodimer on B cells (as well as macrophages and DCs) that binds CD154 (CD40L) on T cells, initiating T cell-mediated activation.
- **Survival Receptors (CD267, CD268, CD269):** CD268 (BAFF receptor) binds BAFF, while CD267 (TACI) and CD269 (BCMA) bind both BAFF and APRIL to mediate critical B-cell survival signals.

## Antigen Presentation and Innate Recognition (APCs)
These molecules are primarily found on Dendritic Cells (DCs) and Macrophages, acting as pattern recognition receptors or facilitating antigen display.
- **CD1a–e:** MHC class I-like molecules that present nonpeptide (lipid and glycolipid) antigens to invariant NKT cells.
- **CD14:** A GPI-linked molecule that binds a complex of LPS and LPS-binding protein to display LPS to TLR4, required for macrophage activation.
- **CD74 (MHC class II invariant chain):** Binds to newly synthesized MHC class II molecules to direct their intracellular sorting.
- **CD80 (B7-1) & CD86 (B7-2):** Key costimulators expressed on DCs, activated B cells, and macrophages that act as the ligands for T-cell receptors CD28 (activating) and CD152 (inhibitory).
- **Carbohydrate Receptors (CD206 & CD207):** CD206 (Mannose receptor) is a C-type lectin on macrophages that mediates endocytosis of terminal mannose-containing glycoproteins and phagocytosis of pathogens. CD207 (Langerin) is a C-type lectin on Langerhans cells that binds mannose, fucose, and N-acetylglucosamine for antigen uptake.
- **CD369 (DECTIN 1):** A pattern recognition receptor specific for fungal and bacterial cell wall glucans.

## Leukocyte Adhesion and Migration Molecules
These molecules mediate physical cell-cell interactions and homing to specific tissues.
- **The Integrins (CD11 & CD18):** CD18 (the β2 integrin chain) noncovalently links with CD11a to form LFA-1, CD11b to form MAC-1, or CD11c to form p150,95. These are responsible for cell-cell adhesion, binding to endothelium, and phagocytosis of iC3b-coated particles. Furthermore, CD29 links with CD49a-d to form VLA (β1) integrins (like VLA-4, CD49d) which mediate leukocyte adhesion to extracellular matrix and endothelium.
- **The Selectins (CD62E, CD62L, CD62P):** CD62E (E-selectin) on endothelial cells and CD62P (P-selectin) on platelets/endothelium promote leukocyte homing to inflamed tissues. CD62L (L-selectin) on leukocytes mediates the homing of naive T cells into lymph nodes.
- **Intercellular Adhesion Molecules (ICAMs):** CD54 (ICAM-1) and CD102 (ICAM-2) are Ig superfamily ligands for the CD11aCD18 (LFA-1) integrin. CD54 also serves as the receptor for rhinovirus.
- **CD31 (PECAM-1):** Heavily involved in leukocyte transmigration through the endothelium.
- **CD106 (VCAM-1):** Mediates cell adhesion to endothelium and serves as the receptor for the CD49dCD29 (VLA-4) integrin.

## Fc Receptors (Antibody Binding)
These receptors bind to the fragment crystallizable (Fc) region of various immunoglobulins to trigger distinct effector mechanisms.
- **CD16a & CD16b (FcγRIII):** Bind the Fc region of IgG. CD16a on NK cells and macrophages drives antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and phagocytosis. CD16b is a GPI-linked version on neutrophils that synergizes in immune complex-mediated activation.
- **CD32 (FcγRII):** An inhibitory receptor with an ITIM on B cells and macrophages that binds aggregated IgG to block activation signals.
- **CD64 (FcγRI):** The high-affinity receptor for IgG on monocytes and macrophages, heavily involved in phagocytosis and ADCC.
- **CD23 (FcεRII):** The low-affinity receptor for IgE, induced by IL-4.
- **CD89 (FcαR):** Binds IgA and C-reactive protein.

## Complement System Receptors and Regulators
These molecules control the powerful complement cascade or recognize complement fragments.
- **CD35 (CR1):** Binds C3b and C4b to promote the phagocytosis of coated particles and regulate complement activation.
- **CD46 (MCP) & CD55 (DAF):** Both are broad-spectrum regulators of complement activation.
- **CD59:** A GPI-linked molecule that specifically binds C9 to inhibit the formation of the terminal complement membrane attack complex.
- **CD88 (C5a receptor):** A G protein-coupled receptor that binds the C5a fragment, playing a major role in complement-induced inflammation.

## Natural Killer (NK) Cell Signatures
These C-type lectins and Ig superfamily molecules govern the activation or inhibition of NK cells based on host MHC class I display.
- **CD94:** Covalently assembles with NKG2 molecules; the CD94/NKG2 complex functions as an inhibitory receptor by binding to HLA-E MHC class I molecules.
- **CD158 (KIR):** Killer cell Ig-like receptors possessing either ITIMs or ITAMs, dictating the inhibition or activation of NK cells upon interaction with appropriate HLA class I molecules.
- **CD159a (NKG2A) & CD159c (NKG2C):** CD159a pairs with CD94 for inhibition/activation, whereas CD159c pairs with CD94 specifically for activation upon interacting with HLA class I.
- **CD244 (2B4) & CD314 (NKG2D):** CD244 modulates NK cell cytolytic activity by acting as a receptor for CD148, while CD314 (NKG2D) binds MHC class I and class I-like molecules (MIC-A, MIC-B) to drive activation.

## Key Apoptotic, Regulatory, and Broad Markers
This final grouping contains universal cell markers, cytokine receptors, and death pathways.
- **CD45 (Leukocyte Common Antigen):** A tyrosine phosphatase expressed in multiple isoforms (CD45RO on memory T cells, CD45RA on naive T cells) that regulates T- and B-cell activation.
- **CD25 (IL-2 receptor α chain):** Associates with IL-2Rβ and IL-2Rγ to form the high-affinity IL-2 receptor on activated T/B cells and regulatory T cells (Treg), promoting responses to low concentrations of IL-2.
- **CD47:** Widely expressed on hematopoietic and other cells, acting as a crucial "Don't eat me" signal to phagocytes, while also participating in leukocyte adhesion and migration.
- **CD95 (FAS) & CD178 (FAS Ligand):** CD178 is expressed on activated T cells and binds CD95 to deliver signals that trigger apoptotic death.
