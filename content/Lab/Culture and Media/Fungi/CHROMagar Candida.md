### Definition and Core Purpose

- **Selective Chromogenic Medium**: CHROMagar Candida is a specialized selective culture medium designed for the simultaneous primary isolation and rapid presumptive identification of medically important **_Candida_ species** directly from clinical specimens.
- **Inhibition of Background Microbiota**: Incorporates selective antibacterial agents that suppress background bacterial microbiota, enhancing diagnostic sensitivity and allowing yeast pathogens to be easily recognized.

---

### Biochemical Mechanism

- **Chromogenic Substrates**: Formulated with specific chromogenic enzyme substrates that react with distinct fungal enzymes characteristic of selected yeast species.
- **Enzymatic Cleavage and Color Development**: Differential enzymatic breakdown of the chromogens yields species-specific color changes within the fungal colonies, enabling visual differentiation based on colonial morphology and color.

---

### Colonial Appearance and Species Differentiation

Following incubation for **1 to 4 days**, _Candida_ species produce distinct colonial colors and morphologic features:

- _**Candida albicans**_: Forms characteristic **green colonies**.
- _**Candida tropicalis**_: Forms **blue to blue-gray colonies**.
- _**Candida krusei**_: Forms **large, rough, pale pinkish colonies**.
- _**Candida glabrata**_: Forms **dark purple to mauve colonies**.
- **_Candida parapsilosis_, _Candida lusitaniae_, and _Candida guilliermondii_**: Produce colonies with a **pinkish to mauve hue**.

---

### Diagnostic Utility and Clinical Advantages

- **Detection of Mixed Infections**: Facilitates the immediate visual detection and recovery of mixed _Candida_ species within a single clinical specimen.
- **Direct Specimen and Blood Culture Processing**: Can be inoculated directly with clinical specimens or samples from positive blood culture bottles, shortening turnaround times for preliminary identification and aiding direct fluconazole susceptibility determination.
- **Integration with Auxiliary Assays**: May be coupled with supplementary biochemical tests, such as the rapid trehalose test (RAT) for confirming _C. glabrata_.
- **Specialized Formulations**: Formulations such as **CHROMagar Candida Plus** have been developed specifically to identify the emerging, multidrug-resistant pathogen _**Candida auris**_ directly from clinical material.
