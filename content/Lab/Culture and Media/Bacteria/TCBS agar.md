**TCBS Agar (Thiosulfate-Citrate-Bile Salts-Sucrose Agar)** is a specialized selective and differential solid culture medium designed for the primary isolation, recovery, and rapid differentiation of pathogenic **_Vibrio_ species**—including _Vibrio cholerae_, _Vibrio parahaemolyticus_, and _Vibrio vulnificus_—from clinical stool specimens and seafood or environmental samples.

---

### Selective Function and Mechanisms

- **Alkaline Environment**: Formulated with an elevated pH (approximately 8.5 to 9.5), which strongly favors the proliferation of alkali-tolerant vibrios while inhibiting acid-sensitive enteric flora.
- **Selective Inhibitory Agents**: Incorporates **bile salts**, **sodium thiosulfate**, and **sodium citrate**. These components inhibit Gram-positive organisms and suppress most non-vibrio Gram-negative intestinal commensals (coliforms and enterics) present in fecal specimens.

---

### Differential Principles

1. **Sucrose Fermentation**:
    - Contains **sucrose** as the fermentable carbohydrate substrate alongside pH indicator dyes (such as bromothymol blue and thymol blue).
    - Organisms that ferment sucrose generate acidic metabolic by-products, causing the pH indicator in the surrounding agar to shift from green to yellow.
2. **Non-Sucrose Fermentation**:
    - Organisms incapable of fermenting sucrose utilize alternative nutrient sources without generating sufficient acid to shift the pH indicator, leaving the colonies and surrounding medium green.

---

### Colonial Morphology and Interpretation

- _**Vibrio cholerae**_:
    
    - Ferments sucrose rapidly.
    - Yields smooth, glistening, convex **yellow colonies** (2 to 3 mm in diameter) surrounded by a diffuse yellow zone or halo in the medium against the dark-green background of the agar.
- **_Vibrio parahaemolyticus_ and _Vibrio vulnificus_**:
    
    - Do not ferment sucrose.
    - Form characteristic **green colonies** on TCBS agar.

---

### Clinical Workflow and Diagnostic Application

- **Primary Plating & Enrichment Synergy**: Stool specimens from patients suspected of having cholera or Vibrio-associated gastroenteritis are inoculated directly onto TCBS agar or subcultured onto TCBS following preliminary enrichment in **alkaline peptone broth** (pH ~8.5–8.6) for 6 to 8 hours.
- **Preliminary & Confirmatory Screening**: Typical colonies picked from TCBS agar within 18 hours of incubation undergo rapid screening via the **oxidase test** (vibrios are oxidase-positive) followed by slide agglutination with specific anti-O polyvalent antisera for serogroup confirmation.
