**Hektoen Enteric (HE) Agar** is a selective and differential culture medium designed for the isolation and identification of Gram-negative enteric pathogens—specifically _Salmonella_ and _Shigella_ species—from fecal specimens and other clinical samples containing normal microbial flora.

---

### Selective Function

- **Inhibitory Agents**: Formulated with bile salts to inhibit the growth of Gram-positive organisms and suppress many non-pathogenic, commensal Gram-negative intestinal bacteria.
- **Recovery of Enteric Pathogens**: By retarding the growth of competing normal intestinal flora, HE agar favors the selective recovery and isolation of _Salmonella_ and _Shigella_ species.

---

### Differential Mechanisms

1. **Carbohydrate Fermentation**:
    - Contains three fermentable carbohydrates: **lactose**, **sucrose**, and **salicin**.
    - Organisms that ferment one or more of these carbohydrates produce acidic metabolic end-products, causing pH indicator dyes in the agar to shift color.
2. **Hydrogen Sulfide (\(\text{H}_2\text{S}\)) Production**:
    - Formulated with sodium thiosulfate and **ferric ammonium citrate**.
    - Organisms capable of producing \(\text{H}_2\text{S}\) reduce thiosulfate; the resulting gas reacts with ferric ammonium citrate to form an insoluble black iron sulfide precipitate.

---

### Colony Morphology and Interpretation

- **_Shigella_ Species**:
    
    - Do not ferment lactose, sucrose, or salicin and do not produce \(\text{H}_2\text{S}\).
    - Appear as **translucent, colorless to blue-green colonies** on HE agar.
- **_Salmonella_ Species**:
    
    - Do not ferment the carbohydrate substrates present in HE agar but produce \(\text{H}_2\text{S}\).
    - Form characteristic **blue-green colonies with black centers** or predominantly **black colonies** due to ferric ammonium citrate precipitation.
- **Lactose / Sucrose / Salicin Fermenters** (e.g., _Escherichia coli_ and other coliforms):
    
    - Ferment carbohydrates to produce acid, turning the colonies and surrounding medium **bright yellow, orange, or salmon-pink**.

---

### Clinical Workflow

- **Primary Plating & Enrichment**: Stool specimens are streaked directly onto HE agar or subcultured onto HE agar following enrichment in liquid selective media (such as selenite F broth).
- **Subculture for Confirmation**: Non-lactose-fermenting suspect colonies (colorless or black-centered) are picked from HE agar and inoculated onto Triple Sugar Iron (TSI) agar slants or subjected to definitive identification via slide agglutination antisera and mass spectrometry.
