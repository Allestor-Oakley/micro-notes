### Definition and General Properties

- **Enriched Complex Medium**: Brain-heart infusion (BHI) agar/medium is a nutrient-rich, complex culture medium used in diagnostic medical microbiology.
- **Growth Support**: It provides essential nutrients to support the cultivation of a broad spectrum of microorganisms, ranging from non-fastidious bacteria to fastidious pathogens, obligate anaerobes, and fungi.

---

### Key Applications and Target Organisms

#### 1. Cultivation of Fastidious and Anaerobic Bacteria

- **Obligate Anaerobes**: BHI agar serves as a core complex medium for recovering anaerobic bacteria. For optimal growth of obligate anaerobes, it is frequently supplemented with additives such as **hemin**, **vitamin K1**, and **blood**.
- **_Actinomyces_ Species**: Incubation of _Actinomyces_ on BHI agar for 72 hours typically yields colonies approximately 2 mm in diameter that exhibit a characteristic **"molar tooth"** morphology.
- _**Yersinia pestis**_: Cultured on solid media or in BHI broth. In BHI liquid broth, _Y. pestis_ characteristically grows as visible **clumps of cells at the bottom of the tube**.
- **_Brucella_ Species**: BHI media supports the growth of _Brucella_ species alongside other enriched general media like trypticase-soy agar.
- **_Helicobacter_ Species**: Biopsy specimens evaluated for _Helicobacter_ species can be inoculated onto BHI-enriched Columbia blood agar formulations.

#### 2. Diagnostic Mycology

- **Recovery of Yeasts and Molds**: BHI agar—formulated with or without selective antibiotics, or prepared as BHI blood agar slants—is widely utilized for culturing pathogenic yeasts and molds, including dimorphic fungi.
- **Replacement of Traditional Media**: In routine diagnostic mycology, BHI agar and inhibitory mold agar (IMA) have largely replaced the traditional use of **Sabouraud's dextrose agar** for isolating fungi from clinical specimens.
