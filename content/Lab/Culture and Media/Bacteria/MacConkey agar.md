### Classification and Purpose

- **Complex Culture Medium**: MacConkey agar is a complex, selective, and differential culture medium used for the primary isolation and cultivation of Gram-negative enteric bacilli (members of the order Enterobacterales).
- **Specimen Processing**: It is routinely employed to process clinical specimens collected from non-sterile sites normally contaminated with resident microbiota, such as stool, feces, and sputum.

### Selective Mechanism

- **Inhibitory Agents**: Formulated with bile salts and dyes (such as crystal violet).
- **Target Organisms**: Bile salts and dyes inhibit the growth of Gram-positive bacteria and Gram-negative cocci while selecting for bile-resistant Gram-negative rods that typically inhabit the gastrointestinal tract.
- **Limitations**: Certain fastidious or sensitive Gram-negative organisms, such as _Pasteurella multocida_, grow poorly on MacConkey agar.

### Differential Mechanism

- **Substrate and Indicator**: Contains **lactose** as a fermentable carbohydrate substrate and a pH indicator dye (neutral red).
- **Lactose-Fermenting Organisms (Coliforms)**:
    - Bacteria that ferment lactose produce acidic metabolic end-products, decreasing the pH of the medium.
    - The pH shift causes colonies and surrounding agar to turn **pink-red to pink-purple**.
    - _Examples_: _Escherichia coli_, _Klebsiella_ species, _Enterobacter_ species, _Citrobacter_ species, and _Serratia_ species.
- **Non-Lactose-Fermenting Organisms**:
    - Organisms incapable of fermenting lactose utilize peptones for energy, producing non-acidic end-products.
    - Form **colorless, pale, translucent, or white nonpigmented colonies**.
    - _Examples_: _Salmonella_ species, _Shigella_ species, _Yersinia_ species, and _Proteus_ species.

### Diagnostic Application and Specialized Formulations

- **Rapid Screening**: Enables rapid preliminary differentiation between potential non-lactose-fermenting enteric pathogens (_Salmonella_, _Shigella_) and lactose-fermenting commensals or coliforms.
- **Sorbitol MacConkey (SMAC) Agar**:
    - Formulated by replacing lactose with **D-sorbitol**.
    - Used specifically for screening pathogenic _Escherichia coli_ O157:H7, which fails to ferment sorbitol (producing colorless/clear colonies), whereas most other _E. coli_ strains ferment sorbitol.
