**Middlebrook 7H11 agar** is a semisynthetic, chemically defined, agar-based culture medium used for the primary isolation, cultivation, colony morphology observation, and antimicrobial susceptibility testing of **_Mycobacterium_ species**, including _Mycobacterium tuberculosis_ and nontuberculous mycobacteria (NTM).

---

### Composition and Role of Key Ingredients

- **Defined Mineral Base**: Formulated with defined mineral salts, vitamins, cofactors, and **glycerol**, which serves as an essential carbon and energy source for mycobacterial metabolism.
- **Casein Hydrolysate**: Contains **casein hydrolysate** (an enzymatic digest of casein), which supplies additional amino acids and peptide nitrogen to stimulate growth.
- **Oleic Acid–Albumin–Dextrose–Catalase (OADC) Supplement**:
    - **Albumin**: Functions as a detoxifying agent that binds and neutralizes toxic free fatty acids present in clinical specimens or the medium.
    - **Oleic Acid**: An essential fatty acid utilized in mycobacterial metabolic pathways.
    - **Catalase**: Decomposes toxic peroxides that can form in the medium during preparation or incubation.
    - **Dextrose**: Provides a supplementary carbohydrate energy source.

---

### Key Features and Comparison to Egg-Based Media

- **Casein Hydrolysate vs. Middlebrook 7H10**: The primary distinction between Middlebrook 7H10 and 7H11 agar is the inclusion of casein hydrolysate in 7H11, which enhances the recovery rate of fastidious or drug-resistant strains of _M. tuberculosis_.
- **Transparent Agar Base**: Unlike opaque egg-based media (such as Löwenstein–Jensen medium), Middlebrook 7H11 is a clear, agar-based medium. Its optical clarity permits early microscopic observation of microcolonies and micro-cording under low-power magnification before growth is visible to the naked eye.
- **Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing**: Utilized in reference laboratories for quantitative agar-proportion drug susceptibility testing, as antibiotics do not bind to egg proteins in this defined agar system.

---

### Selective Formulations

- **Antibiotic Supplements**: Selective versions of Middlebrook 7H11 are formulated by incorporating antimicrobial agents (such as polymyxin B, amphotericin B, carbenicillin, and trimethoprim). These additives inhibit the overgrowth of contaminating bacteria and fungi during the processing of non-sterile clinical specimens (e.g., decontaminated sputum).

---

### Cultivation and Incubation Parameters

- **Incubation Conditions**: Inoculated plates or slants are incubated aerobically at **35°C–37°C** in an atmosphere supplemented with **5%–10% CO₂**.
- **Parallel Processing Protocol**: Diagnostic protocols recommend inoculating selective/nonselective Middlebrook 7H11 agar in parallel with liquid broth culture systems (such as MGIT) and egg-based media (Löwenstein–Jensen) to optimize isolation rates.
