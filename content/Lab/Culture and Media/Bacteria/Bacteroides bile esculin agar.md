**Bacteroides bile esculin (BBE) agar** is a selective and differential culture medium used in clinical microbiology for the isolation and rapid identification of obligate anaerobes, specifically members of the _Bacteroides fragilis_ group.

### Selective Function

- **Bile**: Most aerobic and anaerobic bacteria are inhibited by bile; however, growth of organisms in the genus _Bacteroides_ (such as the _B. fragilis_ group) is stimulated by bile.
- **Gentamicin**: Included as an antimicrobial agent to inhibit most other aerobic and anaerobic microorganisms, as members of the _B. fragilis_ group are resistant to gentamicin.

### Differential Function

- **Esculin Hydrolysis**: The medium contains esculin as a differential substrate.
- **Black Precipitate**: Organisms belonging to the _B. fragilis_ group hydrolyze esculin, resulting in the formation of a distinct black precipitate in the agar surrounding the bacterial colonies.

### Clinical Utility

- BBE agar suppresses non-target mucosal flora and facilitates the isolation and identification of _B. fragilis_ group pathogens from polymicrobial clinical specimens.
