### California encephalitis virus

#### Molecular Virology & Structure
*   **Genomic Architecture:** Contains a segmented, circular, single-stranded negative-sense (and ambisense) RNA genome. The genome is divided into three distinct segments: Large (L), Medium (M), and Small (S).
*   **Capsid & Nucleocapsid:** N/A
*   **Envelope Properties:** Enveloped virus. The envelope contains a spike complex consisting of the Gn and Gc glycoproteins.
*   **Virion Size & Physical Morphology:** Medium-sized, generally spherical virion measuring 80 to 120 nm in diameter.
*   **Structural & Non-Structural Proteins:** Encodes its own RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (L segment, 170 kDa). The M segment encodes the Gc glycoprotein (75 kDa), Gn glycoprotein (65 kDa), and NSm nonstructural protein (15-17 kDa). The S segment encodes the N structural nucleocapsid protein (25 kDa) and NSs nonstructural protein (10 kDa).

#### Viral Replication Cycle
*   **Attachment, Host Receptors & Tropism:** Exhibits tropism for the central nervous system, brain, and neurons.
*   **Entry Mechanism:** N/A
*   **Uncoating & Intracellular Trafficking:** N/A
*   **Transcription & Translation Strategy:** Replicates entirely in the host cell cytoplasm. Encodes an RNA-dependent RNA polymerase to facilitate its own replication.
*   **Assembly, Maturation & Egress:** N/A

#### Pathogenesis & Host Immune Evasion
*   **Direct Cytopathic Effects:** N/A
*   **Latent Infection & Reactivation Triggers:** N/A
*   **Oncogenesis Mechanisms:** N/A
*   **Immune Evasion Strategies:** N/A
*   **Host Defense & Immunodeficiency Links:** Antibody responses can successfully block the disease. The characteristic prodrome of flulike symptoms is directly caused by the host's own interferon and cytokine responses to the infection.

#### Clinical Syndromes
*   **Primary Diseases:** California encephalitis, which typically presents as a febrile illness, encephalitis, and rash.
*   **Secondary, Atypical & Alternate Presentations:** Aseptic meningitis.
*   **Incubation Period:** Generally 4 to 21 days for arboviral encephalitides.
*   **Classic Prodrome:** Sudden onset of a flulike illness (driven by interferon and cytokine responses) presenting with fever, severe headache, chills, nausea, vomiting, generalized pains, malaise, and a stiff neck.
*   **Pathognomonic Signs:** N/A
*   **Complications & Sequelae:** Severe cases may progress to marked drowsiness, stupor, mental confusion, tremors, convulsions, coma, and flaccid or spastic paralysis. The mortality rate is low (approximately 1%), and long-term sequelae are rare.

#### Epidemiology & Transmission
*   **Reservoir:** Small mammals and rodents (e.g., chipmunks, squirrels, rabbits, marsupials).
*   **Transmission Route:** Zoonotic transmission via the bite of an infected [[Mosquitoes|mosquito]].
*   **Vectors:** [[Mosquitoes]], specifically of the *Aedes* genus (e.g., *Aedes triseriatus*).
*   **Public Health & Isolation:** Endemic primarily in North America (United States and Canada). Most clinical cases occur in patients less than 20 years of age. Transmission is prevented by reducing exposure to the [[Mosquitoes|mosquito]] vector (e.g., draining breeding spots, spraying to kill [[mosquitoes]], and avoiding wooded areas).

#### Advanced Laboratory Diagnostics
*   **Histopathology & Inclusion Bodies:** N/A
*   **Molecular Testing:** Reverse transcriptase-polymerase chain reaction (RT-PCR) genome analysis of cerebrospinal fluid or blood is highly sensitive for detecting the virus.
*   **Serology & Seroconversion Windows:** Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) is utilized to detect specific antibodies.
*   **Cell Culture & Assays:** N/A

#### Pharmacology, Prevention & Prophylaxis
*   **Antiviral Targets & First-Line Agents:** N/A (There is no specific antiviral therapy currently approved for routine treatment).
*   **Resistance Mechanisms & Mutations:** N/A
*   **Treatment Nuances & Timing Constraints:** Treatment is strictly supportive and symptomatic.
*   **Prophylaxis & Vaccine Protocols:** No human vaccine is available. Prevention relies heavily on regional [[Mosquitoes|mosquito]] control and personal protection against [[Mosquitoes|mosquito]] bites.
