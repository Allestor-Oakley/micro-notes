#### Clinical Syndromes & Complications
*   **Acute Migration Phase Presentation:** Direct migration of worms over the cornea and inside conjunctival sacs produces acute eye irritation, excessive lacrimation (tearing), intense itching, pain, and a distinct sensation of a foreign body in the eye.

*   **Pathognomonic Signs:** Direct visual identification and mechanical extraction of a living roundworm from the ocular conjunctival sac or cornea in a patient presenting with excessive lacrimation and a foreign object sensation in the eye.
